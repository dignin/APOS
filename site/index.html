FROM FIRST ROUND TO LAST CALL

A familiar rhythm.
A clearer record.

One shared local POS computer for staff. A private view of the tab for each patron.

  1. 01 / OPEN

    Give every order a home.

    Open a running tab and add items from your product catalog. Keep member and non-member guest profiles organised.

  2. 02 / KEEP TRACK

    Let guests follow along.

    Share a private viewing code or QR link. Optionally enable guest ordering for eligible menu items.

  3. 03 / SETTLE

    Pay a little, or pay it all.

    Record partial or full payments after collecting cash or payment through an external card terminal.

A catalog you can maintain

Edit products and independent EUR/USD prices. Historical orders retain the names and prices recorded at purchase.

Guest access with an end point

Private access expires 24 hours after full settlement, or after a settled tab is successfully submitted by email when mail is configured.

Reports for the bigger picture

Use aggregate reports and exports that omit member names, IDs and guest viewing codes.

Built for your own setup

Run APOS locally with Python 3.10 or newer. Configure a reachable guest address and HTTPS for real guest access.

Before operational use

APOS is not TSE-certified and is not ready as a standalone German fiscal register. Review the project's documented fiscal, receipt, privacy and service limitations for your association before deployment. APOS records payments collected separately; it does not process card payments.
